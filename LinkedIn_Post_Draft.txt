Excited to share my first Data Science & Analytics project! 📊

I completed Task 1 — Business Sales Performance Analytics — as part of my Data Science & Analytics Internship with Future Interns.

I analysed 9,994 Superstore sales transactions (2014–2017) to answer real business questions: which products make money, how sales grow over time, and which regions/categories are truly profitable.

Key insights I found:
• Sales grew 51.4% in 3 years to $733,215 in 2017, but overall margin is only 12.47%
• Technology is the profit engine (17.40% margin) — Copiers alone earn at 37.20% margin
• Furniture sells a lot but earns almost nothing (2.49% margin) — Tables actually lose -$17,725
• Discounts above 20% destroy profit — discounts above 50% lost -$76,559
• Texas is 3rd in sales but loses -$25,729 — high revenue, negative profit

My top recommendation: Cap discounts at 20%, fix the Tables line, and push Technology in Q4 (November is the peak month every year).
